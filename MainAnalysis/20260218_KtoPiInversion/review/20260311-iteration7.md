### 1. High: the `dNch/deta` refolding figure and its supporting Markdown now summarize different observables, and the published figure range clips the actual species-level failure

Relevant files:

- `overleaf_repo_git/main.tex:375-378`
- `report/20260310_dndeta_unfolding_update.md:97-116`
- `overleaf_repo_git/report/20260310_dndeta_unfolding_update.md:97-116`
- `make_unfolding_validation_plots.py:123-124`
- `make_unfolding_validation_plots.py:386-398`
- `runDNdEtaUnfolding_BayesSVD.C:535-544`

What is happening:

- The refolding figure in the note is explicitly a species-level plot:
  kaon and pion reco yields are compared to refolded kaon and pion reco yields.
- The plotting script confirms that this figure is built from
  `hKMcReco`, `hKMcBayesRefold_dNdEta`, `hPiMcReco`, and
  `hPiMcBayesRefold_dNdEta`.
- But the current Markdown status notes summarize the refolding quality using the
  combined ratio-level histograms `hRefoldRecoClosureMc_dNdEta` and
  `hRefoldRecoClosureData_dNdEta`, which are constructed only after taking the
  refolded `K/\pi` ratio divided by the reco `K/\pi` ratio.

What I verified from the current ROOT file
`output/systematics_20260306_dndeta/nominal_unfold_dndeta.root`:

- the combined ratio-level refolding RMS values are indeed small:
  `0.01724` for MC and `0.03401` for data
- but the species-level refolding ratios used in the figure are not small:
  - K MC reaches `0.1054` in the final bin
  - K data reaches `0.1229` in the final bin
  - Pi MC reaches `0.1105` in the final bin
  - Pi data reaches `0.1118` in the final bin
  - the drop is already severe before the final bin, e.g. around `0.57` by bin 7

Why this matters:

- The note caption currently says the species-level reco-space agreement is now
  good across the populated bins, with the remaining deviation concentrated in
  the final merged overflow bin.
- That is not what the species-level histograms show.
- On top of that, the plotting macro hard-codes the ratio panel to `0.90-1.10`,
  so the large tail failures are clipped out of view in the published figure.

Bottom line:

- The current `dNch/deta` validation package is internally inconsistent:
  the figure is species-level, the summary numbers are ratio-level, and the plot
  range hides the species-level disagreement.

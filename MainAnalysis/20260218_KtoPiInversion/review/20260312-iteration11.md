### 1. High: the Discussion and Conclusions overstate what the current measurement establishes

Relevant files:

- `overleaf_repo_git/main.tex:637-643`
- `overleaf_repo_git/main.tex:651-666`
- `overleaf_repo_git/main.tex:774-831`
- `overleaf_repo_git/main.tex:1046-1066`

What is happening:

- The primary quoted observable is a DELPHI-to-MC double ratio.
- The strongest branch is the \(K/\pi\) result versus \(N_{\mathrm{tag}}^{\mathrm{ch}}\).
- The \(dN_{\mathrm{ch}}/d\eta\) branch is still explicitly treated elsewhere in the note as only acceptable at ratio level, with a cautionary merged-overflow tail bin and an open stricter parameter-choice issue.
- Despite that, the Discussion and Conclusions move to fairly strong language such as:
  - "multiplicity-independent hadronization chemistry"
  - "no additional strangeness-enhancement mechanism beyond standard fragmentation is required"

Why this matters:

- Those statements read stronger than what the present note actually demonstrates.
- The current note shows that the DELPHI \(K/\pi\) measurement does not reveal a strong multiplicity dependence beyond the present fragmentation baseline within the quoted precision.
- That is not the same as establishing that \(e^+e^-\) hadrochemistry is generically multiplicity-independent, or that no extra mechanism could appear in other observables or at higher effective parton density.
- This is especially important because the note itself repeatedly reminds the reader that:
  - the \(dN_{\mathrm{ch}}/d\eta\) branch is not fully closed at species level
  - the measured observable is light-flavor \(K/\pi\), not a multi-strange enhancement observable

Requested text adjustment:

- Rephrase the main interpretation more conservatively.
- A defensible version would be:
  - the current DELPHI fiducial \(K/\pi\) measurement shows no clear multiplicity dependence beyond the nominal fragmentation baseline within present uncertainties
  - the present data do not provide evidence for a rising \(K/\pi\) trend analogous to the stronger strange-hadron enhancements discussed in hadronic systems
- I would avoid the stronger claim that no additional strangeness-enhancement mechanism is required by the data.
